# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Agent CodeWalk should be a welcoming place to learn, ask questions, report problems, and
build useful software. We are committed to a community in which people can participate
without harassment, regardless of experience level, identity, background, appearance,
ability, nationality, religion, or technical preference.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas and code, not the person presenting them.
- Assume good intent while still taking impact seriously.
- Welcome questions from people who are new to the project or its tools.
- Give credit, protect private information, and respect reasonable boundaries.
- Accept a maintainer's request to de-escalate, move, or end an unproductive discussion.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, stalking, or sustained personal attacks
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- Deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or sharing private information without permission
- Trolling, repeated disruption, or knowingly false accusations
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional public workspace

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release
channels, and other spaces where someone is representing the Agent CodeWalk project. It
also applies to private communication that directly affects a person's ability to
participate safely in the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

For an immediate public problem, flag the content and disengage; do not start a separate
public investigation. To make a private report, contact a repository maintainer through
their GitHub profile or use the repository's private Security reporting form and begin the
title with `Code of Conduct`.

Include links or screenshots, the approximate time, who was involved, and any immediate
safety concern. Maintainers will limit the report to people needed to review it, avoid
conflicts of interest, and communicate the outcome to the reporter when possible.

Responses may include a private correction, a warning, removal of content, temporary
participation limits, or a permanent ban. Knowingly false or bad-faith reports may receive
the same response. Good-faith reports will not be penalized when the available evidence
does not support enforcement.
